- Retained the six existing scenery choices and all placeable pieces. The new settings use the existing local save and export format.

- Shaped Mountain Cut as two continuous eroded rock walls with visible strata. Snowy Summit now uses a layered ridgeline with exposed gullies and snow collecting on gentler slopes.
- Broke up Island Sanctuary's shoreline with asymmetric coves, wet sand, a shallow-to-deep water gradient, and intermittent surf. The water and shore use the same boundary.
- Kept the saved scenery IDs, placement clearance, and camera source intact.
